Does DrKing replace my practice management software?

No. Your practice management software remains the home for clinical records and billing. DrKing handles the steps before the consultation: capturing enquiries, following up and booking appointments. They complement each other.

Can DrKing connect with our existing PMS?

DrKing connects with practice management software via API. The available connection depends on your PMS and its API access. During a demo, we can confirm compatibility and the booking workflow for your clinic.

Will DrKing store our patients’ clinical records?

No. DrKing never stores clinical data. Patient health records stay in your practice management system. DrKing focuses on enquiries, appointment booking, follow-up and reviews.

Why add DrKing if our dental PMS already books appointments?

A booking calendar is one part of the journey. DrKing also captures new enquiries across channels, answers with AI voice and chat after hours, texts back missed calls and follows up with leads. It helps turn the interest your clinic receives into appointments in your existing workflow.
