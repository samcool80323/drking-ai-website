Talk to the DrKing team
Can it log calls for compliance?

Yes. DrKing automatically logs family interactions, including the enquiry and action taken, to support your compliance records. Record-keeping is one part of meeting aged care standards; your facility remains responsible for its policies, consent, access and retention requirements.

Does it handle urgent vs routine family enquiries?

Yes. DrKing handles routine questions using approved facility information and routes urgent concerns to on-duty staff according to your escalation rules. Your care team stays responsible for clinical assessment and advice.

Can it send updates to families?

Yes. Automated updates can be configured for authorised family contacts using the content and timing your facility approves. Confirm contact permissions and what information can be shared during setup.

Does it work across multiple facilities?

Yes. The multi-facility dashboard brings communication into one view, while each facility can have its own information, staff contacts and escalation pathways.

Can staff see call history per resident?

Yes. Authorised staff can view family communication history associated with a resident, helping them understand previous enquiries and follow-ups. Access and resident matching are configured around your facility’s workflows.
