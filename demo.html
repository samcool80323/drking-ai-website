Here’s what to know before we say hello.

Is the demo really free?

Yes. Your 20-minute DrKing demo is free, with no obligation to buy and no payment details required.

What will we cover in 20 minutes?

We will talk about your clinic, explore where patient enquiries may be slipping through, and show you how DrKing can help turn those opportunities into bookings. There will be time for your questions, too.

Do I need to prepare anything?

No preparation is required. If you know your approximate monthly patient volume and the challenges your team faces, that will help us tailor the conversation. You do not need to share any patient-identifying information.

Can I bring someone from my team?

Absolutely. Your practice manager, front-desk lead, or anyone involved in the patient journey is welcome. Mention who you would like to include when we arrange your demo.

What happens after I request a demo?

The form opens a prefilled email in your email app. Send it to request your demo. We respond within one business hour to arrange a time that works for you.
