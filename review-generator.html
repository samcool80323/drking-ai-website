Let’s have a chat
How are Google review requests sent automatically?

Once your appointment workflow is connected and configured, DrKing sends an SMS after a completed visit. The message includes a direct link to your clinic’s Google review page. Your team chooses the sending window during setup.

Can I choose when patients receive the SMS?

Yes. You can set a post-appointment sending window that suits your clinic and patients. A routine check-up and a treatment that needs recovery time may call for different timing. Your setup can reflect that.

Does DrKing write reviews or guarantee five stars?

No. DrKing automates the invitation, while patients write their own honest reviews and choose their rating. Five-star reviews are the goal, not a guarantee. Google controls whether and when submitted reviews are published.

What happens if a patient has negative feedback?

Private feedback can be surfaced for your team to review and respond to personally. This does not prevent anyone from posting a public review or restrict review invitations to happy patients. DrKing does not remove negative Google reviews.

Can Australian clinics track Google and Facebook reviews together?

DrKing supports Google and Facebook review workflows, with review monitoring to help your team keep track of feedback. During your demo, we’ll walk through the profile connections and appointment workflow for your Australian clinic.
