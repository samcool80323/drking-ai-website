Let's talk
What can I see in DrKing's clinic analytics dashboard?

You can see bookings, cancellations, revenue, and conversion rates by channel, location, and staff member. The dashboard brings these measures together so Australian clinic teams can understand how enquiries become patients and where to focus their attention.

How does source attribution help me understand marketing ROI?

Source attribution connects a tracked lead's origin with its booking and associated revenue. You can identify the channels producing patients and compare that revenue with your channel spend when assessing return on investment. Attribution depends on the source and booking information available to your practice.

Can I compare performance across multiple clinic locations?

Yes. Multi-location comparison lets you review bookings, cancellations, revenue, and conversion rates across your practices. Use the same reporting period to identify differences between locations and discover approaches your wider team could learn from.

Can I see where enquiries drop out before booking?

Yes. The conversion funnel shows how tracked enquiries progress towards a booked appointment. Combined with staff performance metrics, it helps you spot follow-up gaps, understand conversion patterns, and identify where your team may need more support.

Can I choose a custom reporting period?

Yes. Custom date ranges let you focus on the period that matters to your practice, from a recent campaign to a longer review. Your real-time dashboard reflects the latest available data; the timing of individual updates depends on the connected data sources.
