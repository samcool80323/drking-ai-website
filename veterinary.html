Can it handle emergency triage?

Yes. DrKing can gather symptoms and contact details, follow your clinic’s triage protocols, and escalate potential emergencies to your team or nominated emergency vet. It supports the first response; it does not diagnose, prescribe or replace a veterinarian.

Does it know which calls are urgent?

DrKing uses the information a caller provides and your clinic’s escalation rules to flag potentially urgent cases. If urgency is unclear, the call should be escalated for human review rather than treated as routine. Your team sets the protocols and emergency contacts.

Can pet owners book online?

Yes. Pet owners can book routine appointments online using the availability and appointment types your clinic makes available. Potential emergencies are directed to urgent assistance rather than a routine booking.

Does it work for multi-vet practices?

Yes. DrKing can support multi-vet practices with booking rules for different vets, appointment types and locations. Availability and routing are configured for your practice during setup.

Can it send care instructions after visits?

Yes. DrKing can send follow-up care instructions that your veterinary team has approved, along with confirmations and reminders. It shares your guidance; it does not generate a diagnosis or independently change a pet’s treatment plan.
