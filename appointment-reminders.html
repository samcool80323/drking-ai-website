The details behind a better reminder.

When does DrKing send appointment reminders?

The reminder journey starts with an SMS 48 hours before the appointment, followed by a WhatsApp reminder with a location map 2 hours before. SMS reminders can also cover the 2-hour touchpoint. Discuss your preferred channel and timing setup with the DrKing team.

Can patients reschedule directly from an SMS reminder?

Yes. Patients can tap the rescheduling link in their reminder to start changing their appointment. This gives them an easy way to act when plans change and helps your practice hear about it earlier.

What happens if a patient still misses their appointment?

When the appointment is marked as a no-show, DrKing sends an automatic rebooking SMS within 30 minutes. The message invites the patient to book again, helping your team follow up without making another manual call.

Can we customise the wording and language of reminders?

Yes. DrKing supports customisable message templates and multi-language reminders, so messages can reflect your practice’s voice and your patients’ preferences. Confirm the languages and template options you need during your demo.

Are SMS appointment reminders suitable for Australian practices?

DrKing’s SMS and WhatsApp appointment reminders are designed for Australian healthcare practices, including dentists, specialists and allied health providers. They help patients remember appointments and act on changes. The up-to-35% no-show reduction is an indicative benefit, not a guaranteed result; outcomes vary by practice and patient group.
