What to know about building your clinic’s reputation with DrKing.

How does DrKing automate Google review requests?

DrKing helps your clinic send a follow-up invitation that directs patients to your Google review page. The aim is to make asking a consistent part of your workflow. Your demo is the place to confirm request timing, delivery channels and the connections available for your clinic’s systems.

Does DrKing only ask happy patients for reviews?

No. Review invitations should welcome honest feedback from patients regardless of their experience. DrKing’s approach is to make it easier to ask consistently, without offering rewards, requiring a positive rating or directing unhappy patients away from Google.

Can DrKing guarantee a higher Google rating?

No. Patients choose whether to review your clinic and what rating to leave. Consistent requests give more patients the opportunity to share their experience. Neither a particular star rating, search position nor revenue increase is guaranteed.

How do better reviews connect to more bookings?

Reviews can give prospective patients confidence to get in touch. DrKing supports the next steps with automated enquiry responses, AI booking and lead capture, so someone who discovers your clinic has a clear way to start a conversation or request an appointment.

Is this suitable for Australian dental, cosmetic and GP clinics?

DrKing is designed to support patient enquiries and follow-up for Australian clinics, including dental, cosmetic and general practices. In your demo, discuss your existing systems, patient consent process and review-request workflow so you can assess the fit for your practice.
