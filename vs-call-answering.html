How is DrKing different from a traditional answering service?

A traditional message-taking service records a caller’s details so your team can call back. DrKing has a natural conversation, books directly into your calendar and sends a confirmation, completing the booking during the original call.

Is AI call answering cheaper than an answering service?

Traditional answering services often charge per call or through a monthly retainer. DrKing uses a flat monthly fee with unlimited calls. Your savings depend on your current provider and call volume, and completing bookings also reduces the callback work left for your team.

Can DrKing book appointments when our clinic is closed?

Yes. Unlike an after-hours service that only takes messages, DrKing offers full booking capability after hours. Patients can have a conversation, book an available appointment directly into your calendar and receive a confirmation without waiting for your reception team to return.

Will patients have to leave a message or use a phone menu?

DrKing lets patients speak naturally to its AI voice receptionist. For appointment bookings, it handles the conversation and sends a confirmation instead of asking patients to leave their details and wait for a callback. Multi-language support also helps patients communicate in their preferred language.
