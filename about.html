Why we exist

Good care should be easier to reach.

Patients often make first contact when a practice is busy, closed or already stretched. A missed call or slow reply can become a missed opportunity for both the patient and the clinic.

DrKing was built to support the space before the consultation: answering enquiries, helping people take the next step and giving practice teams more room to focus on care.

We do not replace your clinicians or clinical record system. We help the conversations around care work better.

What is DrKing?

DrKing is an AI-powered communication and growth platform built for Australian medical, dental and allied health practices. It helps practices connect with patients and make the path to an appointment easier.

Who is DrKing built for?

It is built for Australian practices that want to improve patient communication, respond more consistently and reduce avoidable administrative pressure.

Does DrKing manage clinical records?

No. DrKing focuses on patient communication and practice growth. It does not replace your clinical record or practice management system.

Does DrKing replace our reception team?

No. It is designed to support your people with routine communication so they have more room for patients and conversations needing a human touch.
