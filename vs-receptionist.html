Is an AI receptionist cheaper than a human receptionist?

A full-time receptionist costs $55,000–$65,000 per year, plus superannuation and leave. DrKing costs a fraction of one salary, with no staffing overhead. Your actual savings depend on your clinic’s staffing needs and how much phone and booking work you hand over.

Will DrKing replace our front-desk team?

DrKing can work alongside your team. It handles repetitive calls, appointment bookings and reminders so your human receptionist can focus on greeting patients and handling complex situations. The right balance depends on your practice.

What happens when several patients call at once, or after hours?

A human receptionist can take one call at a time and usually works set hours. DrKing handles unlimited simultaneous calls and is available 24/7/365, including weekends and public holidays, with no extra cost for after-hours coverage.

Can DrKing book appointments as well as a human receptionist?

DrKing books appointments automatically with real-time calendar sync, reducing manual booking work. Your team can focus on requests that need personal judgement or a more detailed conversation. Explore the AI voice receptionist page for more on how bookings work.
