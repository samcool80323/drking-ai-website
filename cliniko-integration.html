DrKing meets Cliniko.

Your Cliniko calendar keeps the practice running. DrKing keeps it full — answering calls your team can’t get to, texting patients who’ve slipped through, and booking straight into Cliniko.

The problems

What your front desk is actually up against.

Treatment rooms and reception can’t overlap

Your physios, osteos or chiros are with patients. The phone is ringing in an empty or understaffed reception. Someone has to choose: the person in front of you, or the person on the line.

Appointment gaps are invisible until they cost you

A cancelled 2 pm slot means lost revenue and an unbalanced day. Without a system to fill those gaps quickly, they stay empty.

New patients don’t wait

A new patient looking for a physio calls three practices. The first one to answer gets the booking. The second and third get voicemail — and the patient moves on.

The workflows

How DrKing works with Cliniko.

01

Call missed, text sent

DrKing detects the missed call and sends a warm SMS within 60 seconds. The text offers to book an appointment — meeting the patient while they’re still holding their phone.

02

AI books into Cliniko

When the patient replies, DrKing checks your real Cliniko availability and offers actual open slots. The booking lands in your Cliniko calendar automatically.

03

Reminders that reduce no-shows

SMS reminders go out before each appointment. Patients confirm or reschedule with a quick reply — fewer no-shows, fewer empty slots, less chasing for your team.

04

After-hours, covered

Evenings, weekends, early mornings — DrKing answers. Your patients get a real conversation and a confirmed booking, not a voicemail box.

Use cases

Moments where it makes the difference.

01

The patient between sessions

Your whole team is in treatment rooms. A patient calls to reschedule. DrKing answers, moves the appointment to Thursday, and sends a confirmation — your Cliniko calendar updates without anyone leaving a session.

02

The new enquiry at 7 am

A runner tweaks a hamstring on a Saturday morning. They call your practice. DrKing’s voice AI answers, offers Monday’s first available, and the new patient is booked before they’ve finished their coffee.

03

The cancelled slot that refills

A 3 pm cancellation comes in at 11 am. DrKing texts your waitlist, offers the slot, and fills it by 11:20. Your team doesn’t have to think about it.
