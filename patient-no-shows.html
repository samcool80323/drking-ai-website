A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Good questions.
Straight answers.

How much do automated reminders reduce no-shows?

Research shows SMS reminders reduce no-shows by up to 35%. DrKing sends reminders 48 hours and 2 hours before the appointment via SMS and WhatsApp.

Can DrKing fill cancellations from my wait list?

Yes. When a patient cancels, DrKing automatically texts the next patient on your wait list with the newly available slot. First to respond gets it.

Can it collect deposits for high-value appointments?

Yes. DrKing sends text-to-pay links for deposits on implants, ortho, specialist consultations — securing the appointment before it happens.

Does it work for different appointment types?

Yes. DrKing customises reminder timing and content based on appointment type — a cleaning reminder is different from an implant deposit reminder.

What about patients who don't have SMS?

DrKing can send reminders via SMS, WhatsApp, and email. Patients receive reminders on their preferred channel.
