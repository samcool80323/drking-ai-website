What makes DrKing different from a generic chatbot?

A generic FAQ chatbot answers common questions and directs patients to call. DrKing answers questions using your clinic’s services and pricing, books appointments directly in chat, and logs each conversation in your CRM pipeline.

Can DrKing actually book appointments after hours?

Yes. DrKing can book appointments directly in the conversation, 24/7. Patients can move from an enquiry to a confirmed appointment without waiting for your reception team to return a call.

Does DrKing work beyond my clinic’s website?

Yes. DrKing supports web chat, SMS, WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook. It also includes an AI voice receptionist, so your clinic can handle enquiries across more channels than a website-only FAQ chatbot.

Is DrKing suitable for dental and cosmetic clinics?

Yes. DrKing is built for clinics, including dental practices and cosmetic clinics. It is trained on your clinic’s services and pricing, and combines patient enquiries, appointment booking and lead management in one connected experience.
