A little more clarity

Good questions.
Honest answers.

Do I need to wait for an integration to use DrKing?

No. Every DrKing tool runs independently of your PMS. You get AI voice, chat, booking, reminders, reviews, and payments from day one.

What does the Dental4Windows integration include?

Google review request automation is the first planned capability, followed by patient recalls, reactivation, and treatment follow-up. We will announce each capability as it goes live.

How does the waitlist work?

The more practices register interest for a specific PMS, the higher it moves up the priority list. We build integrations in the order Australian clinics ask for them.

Will DrKing affect my PMS data?

No. DrKing does not access or modify your clinical records. We focus on front-end communication and growth. Integrations add capabilities on top, not changes to your PMS.

Can I use DrKing with a PMS not listed here?

Yes. The standalone tools work with any PMS. Register your platform on the waitlist and we will add it to the roadmap.
