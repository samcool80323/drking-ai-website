Clinic decision tool / 02

Find the missed-call gap. Then choose the fix.

A missed call is not automatically a lost patient. Before buying software, check when calls go unanswered, whether callers retry, who owns follow-up, and what the current phone system actually records. This worksheet keeps the decision tied to your clinic’s data.

Your working note↗

Start with what your clinic can verify.

Write down the current process, test the full destination, and give every unresolved question an owner.

Ask for a demonstration →
01 / Define the events

Count calls the same way twice.

Choose the period, each location and its answering hours. Agree what the phone report means by an unanswered attempt before comparing periods. A repeat caller may make several attempts; attempts and people are not interchangeable.

Keep separate counts

  • Inbound attempts eligible for your report.
  • Unanswered before a person or approved service picked up.
  • Abandoned while waiting or during a transfer.
  • Voicemail messages and callback requests.

Exclude or label separately

  • Known spam, test calls and wrong numbers.
  • Repeat attempts by the same caller, if measurable.
  • Calls outside the coverage you intend to assess.
  • Unknown caller intent; do not guess it from a missed ring.

If your phone system cannot distinguish these outcomes, mark the field unknown. Do not manufacture a conversion rate or revenue estimate from incomplete data.

03 / Pick the least complicated fix

What the pattern suggests. What to test next.

These are hypotheses to test, not a promise that one service will solve every gap. Ask your team what happened in the real routing path before changing the technology.

04 / Review the outcome

Measure the next step. Not imagined revenue.

Repeat your worksheet with the same definitions after a change. Record whether the relevant callers reached a person, requested a callback or booked through a verified system. Keep unknown outcomes unknown; a missed attempt does not prove a patient chose another practice.
