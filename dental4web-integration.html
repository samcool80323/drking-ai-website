Integration · Under development

DrKing meets Dental4Web.

If your practice runs Centaur Software’s Dental4Windows or Dental4Web, the DrKing integration is under active development. In the meantime, every DrKing tool — voice AI, text-back, booking, reminders, reviews — works alongside it today.

The problems

What your front desk is actually up against.

Your front desk can’t be in two places at once

The phone rings while a patient is standing at the counter and a dentist is asking for a chart update. Something gives — and it’s usually the caller on the line.

Treatment follow-up is nobody’s job

After a major treatment plan, patients often have questions, concerns or scheduling needs. Without a structured follow-up, those conversations happen ad hoc — or not at all.

Online reputation is an afterthought

Happy patients rarely think to leave a review unless asked. The ask has to be timely, personal and effortless — which is hard when your team is already stretched.

How DrKing works with Dental4Web & Dental4Windows.

Works alongside Dental4Web today

DrKing’s AI voice receptionist, missed-call text-back, online booking, SMS reminders and review invitations run alongside Dental4Web from day one — no integration required to start.

Use cases

Moments where it makes the difference.

01

The missed call that still books

A patient calls during a busy chair-side morning. DrKing’s text-back sends an SMS instantly, the patient replies with a preferred time, and the booking is confirmed — all before your team even notices the missed call.

02

The review that writes itself

A patient finishes a crown prep and leaves happy. DrKing’s review invitation arrives while the good feeling is fresh. The Google review posts before the anaesthetic wears off.

03

The recall list that shrinks

Patients who haven’t been seen in six months receive a friendly text with your current availability. They pick a time. The chair fills. Nobody had to chase.

Honest answers about this integration.

Does DrKing replace my reception team?

No. DrKing handles routine administrative communication — booking, availability, practice information. Complex, sensitive or clinical conversations are flagged for your staff with the full context. Your team stays responsible for what matters most.
