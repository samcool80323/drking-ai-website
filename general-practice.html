A few things you might be wondering

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Something else on your mind?
We’re happy to talk it through.

Does DrKing integrate with Best Practice or Medical Director?

DrKing connects with major GP software via API, subject to your system’s compatibility and access. We handle front-end communication and booking, not clinical records. We’ll confirm the integration available for your practice during your demo.

Can it handle Medicare item numbers?

DrKing’s booking system is Medicare-aware. It can categorise appointment types by item number and guide patients to the right booking type. Your clinic configures these categories and remains responsible for billing decisions.

Can it triage urgent vs routine enquiries?

DrKing helps identify potentially urgent concerns and flags them for immediate staff attention using your clinic’s escalation rules. Routine requests, such as repeat scripts and referrals, can be captured and routed automatically. Clinical assessment and approval stay with your clinicians. For a medical emergency, call 000.

Does it send recall reminders for chronic disease management?

Yes. DrKing automates recall reminders for chronic disease reviews and your clinic’s configured management plans. Recall schedules and appointment terminology follow your current clinical workflows.

Can patients book a specific GP?

Yes. Patients can choose their regular GP or book the first available appointment, based on your clinic’s real-time availability and booking rules.

Does it handle after-hours enquiries?

Yes. DrKing operates 24/7. After-hours patients can book, ask routine questions and receive automated responses. DrKing supports administrative enquiries; it does not replace an after-hours clinical service.

Is it secure?

DrKing is built for front-end communication, with no clinical records stored. Its security approach addresses Data minimisation, Australian Privacy Principles and Australian-focused infrastructure. We can walk you through data handling, access controls and applicable compliance documentation for your clinic.
