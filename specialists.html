Let’s talk about your clinic
Can DrKing capture referrals from fax, email, and online portals?

Yes. DrKing monitors referral channels and captures patient details automatically. Your team reviews and confirms — AI handles the initial patient contact.

Does it manage wait lists?

Yes. When a patient cancels, DrKing automatically contacts the next patient on the wait list and offers the newly available slot.

Can it send pre-appointment questionnaires?

Yes. DrKing sends questionnaires via SMS/email before the appointment, so patients arrive prepared and you save time during the consultation.

Can it track which GPs refer the most patients?

Yes. DrKing’s analytics dashboard shows referral sources, conversion rates, and revenue per referring GP.

Does it work for multi-specialist practices?

Yes. DrKing supports multiple specialists with individual calendars, availability rules, and booking types.

Can it collect consultation fees and procedure deposits?

Yes. Text-to-pay links are sent automatically for consultation fees and procedure deposits.

Is it secure?

Clinical records remain in your practice management system. DrKing’s data handling is reviewed against the Australian Privacy Principles and the requirements agreed for your deployment. Ask us about the applicable controls and responsibilities.
