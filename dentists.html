Does DrKing integrate with Dental for Windows or other dental practice management software?

DrKing connects with your existing PMS via API. We don’t store clinical data — we focus purely on front-end growth: capturing enquiries, booking appointments, and automating communication. We’ll confirm the available integration for your software and version during your demo.

Can patients book a specific dentist?

Yes. DrKing’s booking system supports multi-dentist scheduling. Patients can select their preferred dentist, and the system checks that dentist’s real-time availability.

Can it handle multiple treatment rooms and chairs?

Yes. DrKing manages multiple chairs, multiple dentists, and multiple locations from a single dashboard.

Does it send treatment plan reminders?

Yes. DrKing can automate recall reminders for six-month cleaning, ortho adjustment reminders at two-week intervals, and post-procedure follow-ups.

Can it collect deposits for high-value treatments?

Yes. DrKing sends text-to-pay links for deposits on implants, ortho, and cosmetic treatments — securing the appointment before it happens.

What happens if a patient calls during a procedure?

DrKing AI answers immediately, captures the patient’s details, books the appointment, and sends a confirmation — all while you’re in surgery.

Is it secure and compliant?

Clinical records remain in your practice management system. DrKing’s data handling is reviewed against the Australian Privacy Principles and the requirements agreed for your deployment. Ask us about the applicable controls and responsibilities.
