Ask us something else
Who can become a DrKing referral partner?

If you work with or know Australian clinics, we’d love to hear from you. The program is a natural fit for healthcare consultants, agencies, technology providers and people with trusted clinic relationships. Contact our team to discuss your fit.

How does recurring commission work?

The program pays recurring commission for clinics you refer, subject to the partner agreement. Our team will explain the commission rate, eligible referrals, payment schedule and how long commission continues before you join.

Do I need to sell or support DrKing?

Your role starts with making a relevant introduction. Our team can walk the clinic through DrKing and discuss its needs. We’ll agree on how introductions and ongoing responsibilities work when we set up your partnership.

Which clinics can I refer?

Start with Australian dental clinics and general practices that could benefit from DrKing. If you have another type of clinic in mind, contact us to check whether it’s a good fit.

How do I get started?

Contact the DrKing team and tell us a little about your business and the clinics you work with. We’ll discuss the program, confirm the terms and explain how to submit and track your referrals.
