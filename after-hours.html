What happens when a patient calls after hours?

DrKing AI answers immediately, greets the caller by your clinic name, answers questions about services and availability, and books the appointment — all in real-time, 24/7.

Does it sound like a robot?

No. DrKing AI uses natural conversational speech. Callers can ask questions, change their mind, reschedule — just like talking to a human receptionist.

Can it handle emergency triage?

Yes. DrKing AI identifies urgent symptoms and follows your escalation protocol — either connecting to an on-call clinician or directing to emergency services.

What about public holidays and weekends?

DrKing operates every day, all year. Public holidays, weekends, 3 AM on a Tuesday — every call gets answered.

How does it handle patients who just want to leave a message?

DrKing can take messages too, but most callers prefer to book directly. The AI guides them toward booking rather than leaving a message that might not get returned.
