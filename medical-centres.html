A few things you might be wondering

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Every clinic group works a little differently. Here’s how DrKing brings the pieces together.

Does it support different branding per location?

Yes. Each location can retain its own branding and clinic details while your group manages enquiries, bookings and reporting centrally.

Can patients book at any location?

Yes. Your team can offer available appointments across connected locations, based on the patient’s preferred clinic, practitioner and appointment needs.

Can I see performance by location?

Yes. View calls, enquiries and bookings for an individual clinic or compare locations in one dashboard to understand performance across the group.

Does each location keep its own phone number?

Yes. Existing clinic numbers can connect to a centralised call workflow, so patients can keep calling the number they know. Routing and setup are confirmed during onboarding.

Can I route calls to the nearest location?

Yes. Call routing can direct patients to a suitable nearby clinic using their provided location or preference. Your team sets the routing rules, including opening hours and overflow handling.
