The details behind a calmer, more connected lead pipeline.

Which channels can DrKing capture clinic leads from?

DrKing brings enquiries from web chat, phone, SMS, WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook into one unified CRM pipeline. Your team can see where each enquiry started and follow its progress without jumping between separate inboxes.

How does AI qualify and tag a new enquiry?

DrKing uses the information in an enquiry to identify what the person is looking for, qualify their interest and apply relevant tags. This gives your team context for the next conversation. Lead qualification supports your administrative workflow; it is not clinical assessment or medical advice.

Can leads be assigned to different people in my clinic?

Yes. DrKing can automatically route qualified leads to the right team member using your clinic’s assignment rules. Each enquiry has a clear owner, so reception and other team members know who is responsible for the next step.

Can I see which channels turn enquiries into bookings?

Yes. Lead source tracking shows where enquiries originate, while conversion analytics show how leads move through your pipeline. Together, they help you understand which channels contribute to bookings and where follow-up needs attention.

Is DrKing lead management suitable for Australian GP and cosmetic clinics?

DrKing’s lead management is designed for Australian clinics, including general practices and cosmetic clinics. A unified inbox, automatic assignment and a visual pipeline help teams manage different enquiry types. Book a demo to discuss your clinic’s channels, team structure and workflow.
