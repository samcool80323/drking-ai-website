A few things you might be wondering

Clear answers.

A closer look at bringing your patients back with DrKing.

What is a patient reactivation campaign?

It's a planned check-in with patients who haven't visited your clinic for a while. DrKing identifies lapsed patients and sends personalised SMS invitations to return, then helps handle replies and booking enquiries.

How long should a patient be inactive before we contact them?

Six months is a starting point, rather than a rule for every patient. Your clinic can choose a reactivation window that reflects its usual recall schedule and the type of care you provide. DrKing focuses outreach on patients who fit that window.

What happens when a patient replies to the SMS?

DrKing can respond to routine enquiries and help the patient book an appointment. Interested replies are captured for follow-up, while questions needing your team's judgement can be passed to a person. Appointment reminders can then support the next visit.

Can we choose who receives a campaign?

Define your lapsed-patient audience before sending. Use an eligible patient list with appropriate permission to contact, exclude people who have opted out, and include a clear way to stop future messages. Confirm your clinic's patient data and messaging requirements during setup.

Will our clinic recover $36,000 from one campaign?

That figure is an illustration: 1,200 lapsed patients × 15% reactivation × a $200 attended visit equals $36,000 in gross revenue before costs. It isn't a promised result. Your actual return depends on your patient list, response rate, attendance and visit value. Use the calculator above to explore the same assumptions for your clinic.
