More about reducing no-shows ↗
Does it work with Instagram DMs?

Yes. DrKing captures Instagram enquiries alongside web chat, phone and SMS, so your team can manage conversations in one place and help patients book while they’re interested. Your Instagram account connection is configured during setup.

Can it collect consultation deposits?

Yes. With Text-to-Pay, you can send a secure payment link to collect a consultation deposit. Your clinic sets the deposit amount and cancellation policy, and patients can pay from their phone.

Can it handle multiple treatment types?

Yes. Booking flows can be configured around your clinic’s treatment types, consultation lengths and practitioner availability. Clinical suitability and treatment advice remain with your qualified team.

Does it integrate with cosmetic clinic software?

Integration options depend on your clinic software and the access it provides. During your demo, we’ll review your current system and confirm the supported booking, calendar and patient-management connections before setup.

Can it send before/after review requests?

DrKing can automate post-appointment review requests. If your clinic requests before-and-after photos, patient consent and your clinic’s review process should be part of that workflow. Images and reviews are not automatically published.
