No. DrKing uses natural conversational speech. Callers can ask questions, change their mind, and reschedule, just as they would in a normal conversation.

DrKing handles routine enquiries such as booking, pricing, and availability. Complex calls are flagged for your staff with full context, so they can follow up with the right information.
