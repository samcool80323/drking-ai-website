Before we begin.
A few things you might be wondering.
What happens after I complete the form?
Your email app opens with your intake details ready to review. Add your DrKing contact and send the email to begin your setup. We use your details to set up your AI receptionist, booking system, and follow-up automation within 48 hours.
Do I need to know all my current systems?
No. If you are unsure which phone or booking system you use, enter “Not sure”. If you do not have one, enter “None”. We can clarify the details with you during setup.
Can I use DrKing for more than one location?
Yes. Include your total number of locations in the form so we can understand your business and plan your setup.
What if my email app does not open?
After completing the form, use “Copy intake details” to copy your information. You can then paste it into a new email and send it to your DrKing contact.
Should I include patient or customer information?
No. This form is for your business and contact details only. Please do not include patient records, customer information, passwords, or payment details.
