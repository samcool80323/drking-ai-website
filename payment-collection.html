A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Good questions.
Clear answers.

A little more about making payments easier with DrKing.

How does DrKing help collect unpaid clinic invoices?

DrKing sends patients a secure payment link by SMS, follows up automatically on outstanding invoices and reconciles payments. Your team can spend less time chasing bills and see which invoices still need attention.

Do patients need to download an app to pay?

No. Patients open the payment link from their SMS and complete the payment in their phone's browser. There's no app to download and no need to call reception to pay over the phone.

What happens if a patient doesn't pay after a reminder?

Automatic follow-ups help keep the invoice visible, but payment isn't guaranteed. Your team can review outstanding invoices and decide when a personal conversation is needed, including questions about an invoice or payment difficulties.

Are payments through DrKing secure?

DrKing uses PCI-compliant payment processing. Patients enter their payment details through a secure payment link, rather than sending card details in a text message or reading them out to reception.

Can DrKing help with bookings and new patient enquiries too?

Yes. Alongside payment collection, DrKing supports automated responses, AI booking and lead capture. It helps your clinic manage the journey from the first enquiry to an appointment and payment. Explore the solutions for dentists, specialists and general practice to find the right fit.
