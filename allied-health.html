Let’s talk about your practice
Does DrKing handle NDIS billing?

DrKing automates the communication and payment follow-up for NDIS sessions. We don’t process NDIS claims directly — we ensure invoices are sent, followed up, and paid.

Can it book multiple sessions at once?

Yes. When a patient books session 1, DrKing can automatically schedule sessions 2–6 at the appropriate intervals and send reminders before each.

Does it work for solo practitioners?

Absolutely. DrKing is ideal for solo allied health practitioners who don’t have a receptionist. AI handles calls, bookings, and reminders — you focus on treating patients.

Can patients reschedule via SMS?

Yes. Patients can text “reschedule” and DrKing AI offers alternative times — no phone call needed.

Does it send reminders for each session in a treatment plan?

Yes. DrKing sends SMS and WhatsApp reminders before every session in a treatment plan, reducing no-shows by up to 40%. Results vary by practice.

Can it handle multiple practitioners in one practice?

Yes. DrKing supports multi-practitioner scheduling with individual calendars and availability.

Is it secure?

No clinical data is stored. DrKing is designed to support GDPR and Australian Privacy Principles requirements, with HIPAA-ready workflows and PCI-compliant payments. Ask us about the controls and configuration for your practice.
