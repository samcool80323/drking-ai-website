What 24/7 booking means for your patients and your practice.

Can patients book when our practice is closed?

Yes. DrKing gives patients an online booking pathway 24/7, including evenings, weekends and public holidays. Appointments remain subject to your practice's configured booking options and availability. If no suitable appointment is available, patients can leave an enquiry for your team.

Does DrKing work with our existing booking system?

Your booking system and available integrations need to be checked during setup. DrKing can guide patients to your configured booking pathway; direct appointment scheduling depends on the connection available. Your team should confirm compatibility and how appointment availability is handled before launch.

What happens if a patient isn't ready to book?

DrKing can answer routine practice questions and capture the patient's contact details and enquiry for follow-up. Your reception team can then help with appointment suitability or timing during opening hours, rather than starting with an unanswered overnight message.

Is online booking suitable for GPs, dentists and allied health?

Yes. The booking journey can be tailored to your practice's services and appointment pathways. Explore DrKing for general practice, dentists and allied health. Appointment types that need staff review can be directed to reception for follow-up.

Will online booking replace our reception team?

No. DrKing helps with routine questions, online booking guidance and after-hours enquiry capture. Your reception team still handles complex requests, patient support and exceptions. The aim is to reduce repetitive booking calls and give your team more time for people who need personal help.
