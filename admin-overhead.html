What clinic owners and practice managers want to know.

Which clinic admin tasks can DrKing automate?

DrKing helps with routine phone and website enquiries, appointment booking workflows, new patient lead capture, reminders and payment follow-ups. The scope depends on your clinic’s systems and setup. Start with the repetitive tasks that take the most time, then agree which enquiries should go straight to your team.

Does this mean replacing our receptionist?

The aim is to give your receptionist more time for patients. DrKing handles repeatable admin so your team can focus on welcoming people, resolving complex enquiries and keeping the practice running smoothly. You decide how the extra capacity is used.

Will our clinic really save $24,000 a year?

The $24,000 figure is an illustration: 40% of a $60,000 annual salary. It represents the value of time spent on automatable work, not a guaranteed cash saving. Your outcome depends on task volumes, how much work is automated, and AI and implementation costs. The $0.12 per interaction figure is a comparison assumption; confirm applicable pricing and what counts as an interaction with DrKing.

Can DrKing work with our existing booking process?

Your booking process and available system connections need to be reviewed during setup. DrKing’s workflow can be scoped to your practice, including collecting appointment details and passing requests to your team where a direct booking connection is unavailable. Confirm compatibility before committing to a particular workflow.

What happens when a patient needs a real person?

Define handover rules with your clinic during setup, including which enquiries need staff attention and how they are passed on. DrKing can handle routine information and capture context, while your team handles complex, sensitive or clinical questions. It is an admin assistant, not a substitute for clinical judgement.
