LET'S CLEAR THINGS UP

A few locations.
A few questions.

What to know about multi-location clinic management in Australia with DrKing.

How does DrKing bring multiple clinic locations into one dashboard?

DrKing centralises calls, enquiries and booking activity so your team can follow the patient journey across your clinic group. Location context helps you see where each enquiry belongs, while centralised reporting gives you a group-wide view. Your demo can map this workflow to your locations and existing systems.

Can patients book at another location if their preferred clinic is full?

Cross-location booking helps patients find availability at another connected clinic when their preferred location is full. The booking journey should account for the service they need and their location preference. Availability depends on the booking systems and appointment types configured for your group.

What happens when one location misses a patient call?

DrKing’s automated response helps keep the enquiry moving when reception cannot answer. Lead capture brings the enquiry into a shared view so your team can follow up with the right location context, and AI booking can help the patient take the next step.

Can we compare lead and booking performance between clinics?

Yes. Centralised reporting helps you review enquiry and booking activity across your group and compare individual locations. This helps managers identify follow-up gaps and differences in booking performance without piecing together separate location reports.

Can DrKing work with the booking systems our Australian clinics already use?

The right setup depends on your existing booking systems and how each location manages appointments. In your demo, share the systems, services and workflows used across your group so compatibility and configuration can be confirmed before rollout.
